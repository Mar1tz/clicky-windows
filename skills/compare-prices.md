---
name: Compare prices
when: the user wants prices compared or the best deal found for something they want to buy
---

## Steps
1. What exactly they want (the model, the size, new or used).
2. Check at least three shops the user can buy from (see What to know).
3. A short table: the shop, the price, delivery, in stock or not, a link.
4. Say which is the best deal and why. Never buy anything.

- Shops: (e.g. Takealot, Amazon, Makro)
