---
name: Book a meeting
when: the user wants to book, schedule or set up a meeting or call with someone
ask_before: sending the invite
---

## Steps
1. Who, about what, and roughly when. Ask if the request doesn't say who.
2. Open the user's calendar and find free times that fit.
3. Make the event: a clear title, the people, a video link if it's online.
4. Show the user the time and the people, and ask before sending the invite.

## What to know
- Calendar: (e.g. Google Calendar, Outlook)
- Meetings default to 30 minutes, never before 9:00 or after 17:00
