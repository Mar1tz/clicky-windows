---
name: Make a quote
when: the user wants a quote, quotation or estimate made for a customer
ask_before: sending the quote
---

## Steps
1. Who it's for and what the work is. Ask for anything missing.
2. Price each item from What to know. If something has no price there, ask - never invent one.
3. Make a clean PDF quote: the customer, the items, the total, how long the price holds.
4. Show the user the quote and ask before sending.

## What to know
- Prices: (e.g. call-out R450, labour R380/hour)
- Quotes are valid for 30 days
