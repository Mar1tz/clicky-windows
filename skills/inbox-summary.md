---
name: Summarise my inbox
when: the user wants a summary or roundup of their email or inbox
---

## Steps
1. Open the user's inbox and read what arrived in the period asked for (today, if they don't say).
2. Group it: needs a reply, for information, can be ignored.
3. For each that needs a reply: who, what they want, by when.
4. Change nothing - don't mark as read, move or delete.
