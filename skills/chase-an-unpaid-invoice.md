---
name: Chase an unpaid invoice
when: the user wants to follow up, remind or chase a client about an unpaid or overdue invoice
ask_before: sending the reminder
---

## Steps
1. Find the invoice: who, how much, when it was due.
2. Draft a short, friendly reminder: the invoice number, the amount, the due date, how to pay.
   Firmer only if this is the second reminder or later.
3. Show the user the draft and ask before sending.

## What to know
- Tone: friendly and brief
