---
name: Turn what I said into an email
when: the user dictates or says what an email should say and wants it written up properly
ask_before: sending
---

## Steps
1. Take what the user said and write it as a clear email: a subject, a greeting, the points in order, a sign-off.
2. Keep their meaning and every detail; fix only the wording.
3. Leave it as a draft addressed to whoever they named, and ask before sending.
