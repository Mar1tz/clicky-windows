## Steps
1. Read the link or idea the user gave.
2. Write the post for the platform they named (LinkedIn if they didn't): a hook in the first line,
   the point, a call to action. Hashtags only on Instagram and LinkedIn, three at most.
3. Show the user the post. Only post if they say so.

## What to know
- My voice: plain, no buzzwords
