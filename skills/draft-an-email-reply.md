name: Draft an email reply
when: the user wants an email answered or a reply drafted in their tone
ask_before: sending
---

## Steps
1. Read the email being replied to, and the thread before it.
2. Draft the reply in the user's tone (see What to know). Answer every question it asks.
3. Leave it as a draft and tell the user it's ready. Only send if they say so.

## What to know
- My tone: short, warm, no "I hope this email finds you well"
- Sign off: (e.g. Thanks, Dylan)
