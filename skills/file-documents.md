---
name: Rename and file documents
when: the user wants documents or files renamed, organised or filed into folders
---

## Steps
1. Open each document the user points to and work out what it is: who it's from, what, the date.
2. Rename it: YYYY-MM-DD Who - What (e.g. 2026-10-03 Vodacom - Invoice).
3. Move it to the right folder (see What to know). Make the folder if it's missing.
4. List what was renamed and where it went.

## What to know
- Filing folder: (e.g. Documents\Admin, with a folder per year)
