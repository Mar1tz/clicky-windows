---
name: Write up meeting notes
when: the user wants meeting notes, minutes or a summary of a meeting or call written up
---

## Steps
1. Use what the user gives: a recording's transcript, their rough notes, or what they say.
2. Write: who was there, what was decided, the actions (who, what, by when), open questions.
3. Save it as a document in the workspace named YYYY-MM-DD Meeting - Topic, and say where.
