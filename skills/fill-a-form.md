---
name: Fill in a form
when: the user wants a form filled in from their details or from a document
ask_before: submitting the form
---

## Steps
1. Open the form.
2. Fill each field from the document the user gave, or from what Tora knows about them.
3. Leave a field empty rather than guess, and list the ones left empty.
4. Never type a password. Ask before submitting.
