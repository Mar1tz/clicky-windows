---
name: Send an invoice
when: the user wants to invoice or bill a client for work
ask_before: sending the invoice
---

## Steps
1. Work out who it's for and what for. If the request doesn't say, ask - never guess an amount.
2. Open the invoicing app or site the user uses (see What to know). If none is set, make a clean
   PDF invoice in the workspace instead.
3. One line per piece of work: what it was, the hours or quantity, the rate, the line total.
4. Check the total, the client's details and the due date.
5. Show the user the invoice and the total, and ask before sending.

## What to know
- Invoicing app: (e.g. Xero, QuickBooks, Wave - or "make a PDF")
- My rates: (e.g. design R650/hour)
- Payment terms: due in 14 days
